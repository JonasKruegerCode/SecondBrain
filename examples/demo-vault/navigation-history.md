# Navigation stories of Lantern Bay

> Fictional demonstration. The port and its history are invented.

The archive studies imagined accounts of finding the harbor after dark. Some describe stars; others describe lamps, sound, and the silhouette of the headland.

## Two kinds of record

A sketch can show what a narrator considered important. A contemporary observation can show what is visible under stated conditions. They answer different questions and become useful together when their limits are clear.

## A comparison to make

Pair an invented historic horizon sketch with a card from the [[night-sky-survey|night sky survey]]. Ask which landmarks each emphasizes, then consult [[oral-history|oral history collection]] for the language used to describe the shore.

The [[tide-clock|tide clock]] adds another layer: access to the pier changes with water level in the fictional setting. Record the comparison in the [[field-notebook|field notebook]] and catalog it with [[archive-method|archive method]].
