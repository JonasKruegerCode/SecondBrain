# Lantern Bay field atlas

> Fictional demonstration: every place, person, observation, and project in this atlas is invented.

Lantern Bay is an imagined coastal observatory where a small archive connects the night sky, the shore, and the stories people tell about both. Start with a question and follow the links across disciplines.

## Three routes into the atlas

- **Above the water:** [[night-sky-survey|night sky survey]] → [[tide-clock|tide clock]] → [[navigation-history|navigation stories]].
- **Along the shore:** [[salt-marsh|salt marsh listening station]] → [[shellfish-kitchen|shellfish kitchen notebook]] → [[harvest-calendar|harvest calendar]].
- **Inside the archive:** [[mira-quill|Mira Quill]] → [[oral-history|oral history collection]] → [[archive-method|archive method]].

## This season's shared work

The [[lantern-festival|lantern festival]] brings these routes together: a sky walk, a marsh listening station, and a tasting table. The [[field-notebook|field notebook]] keeps observations separate from interpretations so a good story never becomes accidental evidence.
