# Salt marsh listening station

> Fictional demonstration. The marsh, species records, and trial below are invented.

At Lantern Bay's imaginary marsh, the ecology group asks how water movement shapes the soundscape. The project joins quiet listening with careful description.

## Observation protocol

- Record a location code and a broad tide phase from the [[tide-clock|tide clock]].
- Describe audible patterns before naming their source.
- Note wind and nearby activity in the [[field-notebook|field notebook]].
- Mark every uncertain identification as uncertain.

## Invented pilot result

The sheltered inlet sounded different from the open boardwalk, but the trial changed several conditions at once. The team cannot attribute that difference to tide alone.

## Connections beyond ecology

The [[oral-history|oral history collection]] project collects imagined memories of the inlet. The [[harvest-calendar|harvest calendar]] compares ecological rhythms with kitchen traditions. A quiet listening station will be part of the [[lantern-festival|lantern festival]].
