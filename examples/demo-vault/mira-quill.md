# Mira Quill — fictional archivist

> Fictional demonstration. Mira Quill is an invented character.

Mira looks after the imagined Lantern Bay archive. Her favorite question is: “What would another observer need to know to repeat this?” She pairs field notes with the voices of people who remember a place differently.

## Current projects

1. Add source labels to the [[oral-history|oral history collection]] collection.
2. Compare historic wayfinding stories in [[navigation-history|navigation stories]] with the modern [[night-sky-survey|night sky survey]].
3. Prepare a public reading corner for the [[lantern-festival|lantern festival]].

## Working agreement

A recollection is valuable even when it cannot establish a date. Mira records uncertainty explicitly using [[archive-method|archive method]], and keeps her observations in the [[field-notebook|field notebook]].

## A useful tension

The archive wants both rich stories and reproducible records. Those goals meet when a page says what was observed, who interpreted it, and what remains unknown.
