# Harvest calendar — a bridge between records

> Fictional demonstration. Dates and seasons here are narrative devices, not real harvesting guidance.

The calendar connects three ways of talking about time: the tide, the ecology of the shore, and remembered meals. A shared label does not mean the records describe the same event.
