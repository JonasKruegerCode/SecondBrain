# Oral history collection

> Fictional demonstration. Every speaker and quotation in this collection is invented.

The imaginary archive gathers stories about Lantern Bay's pier, meals, and evening walks. A story is stored as a perspective with a context, not automatically as a verified event.

## Sample fragment

An invented speaker remembers carrying a paper lantern to the pier and waiting for the water to quiet. The memory links a family ritual with a changing shoreline; the date remains unknown.

## Catalog fields

- Source type: fictional interview fragment.
- Subject: pier gathering.
- Certainty: remembered sequence; no verified date.
